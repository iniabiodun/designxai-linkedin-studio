# LinkedIn Studio: a Design x AI workshop kit

Build your own LinkedIn Studio with Claude: a page that turns an idea into a post in your voice, shows you how it will look in the feed, and sends the approved post to a table you own. Then two scheduled tasks that read your week and propose ideas while you sleep.

Nothing to install. You need the Claude desktop app, a place to keep your posts, and a piece of work you can talk about for ten minutes.

**Read [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md) first.** It covers setup, the three decisions to make on paper, and the order to run the prompts.

## Who it is for

Anyone who wants to post from their own work and does not. It works with 200 LinkedIn posts or none, with meeting notes or without, with a website or a single colour you like. The prompts read what you have and ask for what you do not.
