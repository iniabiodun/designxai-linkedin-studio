# 04 Article draft (after the session)

Goal: a marked idea becomes a long-form first draft, in your essay voice, on the same page.

This is the takeaway, not a session step. It needs the weekly loop to have run at least once and put an idea on your page that deserves more than a post. Run it then.

It works whether you already publish long-form or want to start. If you have no long pieces yet, the skill builds its first version from the story you told in Step 1 and your approved posts, and improves once real ones exist.

Paste it into the same task as the rest, or into a new task with the rules block from `00-setup.md` pasted first.

---

```
Step 4: one more scheduled task, "Article draft", plus a skill for my long-form voice. Propose first, then wait for "go".

The skill, called <my name or newsletter>-essay:
- Read my three most recent long pieces at <URL, or "none">. Extract the arc (how I enter, where my own evidence comes, where borrowed wisdom comes, whether I name a framework, how I return to the opening, how I end), the voice rules, the bans, and my title shapes. If I have none, build the arc from the story I told in Step 1 and my approved posts, say so, and note in the skill that it should be rebuilt once three long pieces exist.
- Include an interview step: before drafting, ask for my own lived moment. Never invent one.
- Include a research standard: two to four real quotes with links, two or three public cases with figures, a peer, and one of my own earlier pieces if the idea continues it.
- Deliverable: 1,200 to 1,800 words, section headings, inline links, a short research appendix, no notes explaining choices.
- Final checks as a numbered list.
Propose it for saving and embed it in the task prompt so the run does not depend on finding it.

The task, <day and time>, one hour after the idea runs:
1. Read the last three weeks of "Ideas, week of" pages in my folder, or the "Ideas" sheet if that is what I have. An idea is marked when its "Draft:" line says "Article". Also read my approved-posts table; a post is marked when its status is Article. Add that status option if it is missing.
2. Skip anything already drafted.
3. Research each marked item to the skill's standard.
4. Push me three to five interview questions per item and wait for my reply in the run. "skip" drops an item.
5. Write the draft.
6. Write it into the idea's own section: turn the "Draft: Article" line into a toggle called "Draft: <Title>" and put the essay inside it, bold section headings, one paragraph per block, research at the bottom. On a sheet, put it in a "Draft" cell on the idea's row. Never create a new page. Never write a draft into the approved-posts table.
7. Push me the titles and word counts.
```

---

## Why it writes into the toggle

The idea, its research and its draft belong together. A separate page per draft splits them, and the folder fills with pages you have to cross-reference. A toggle on the idea's own section keeps the page readable and the draft one click away.

## Why marking is a word on a line

No new field, no new view. You are already on the page reading the idea. Typing "Article" on the Draft line is the smallest possible act, and the task can find it.

## What "ready" looks like

- Mark one idea, fire the task by hand, answer the questions
- A toggle appears under that idea with a draft inside
- The draft opens in a real place, your evidence comes before anyone else's, and it ends the way your essays end
