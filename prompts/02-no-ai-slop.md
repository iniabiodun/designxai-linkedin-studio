# 02 No AI slop

Goal: a second skill that strips AI tells from every draft, after the voice skill has written it.

The voice skill says what you sound like. This one says what an AI sounds like, so every draft can be checked against it. They are separate on purpose. Your voice changes as you write more. The list of AI tells does not. The Studio runs them as two passes.

Paste this after the voice skill is saved.

---

```
Step 2: the no-ai-slop skill. Propose first, then wait for "go".

Propose a skill called no-ai-slop: a plain checklist of AI tells to remove from any draft, run as a last pass after writing. Two passes.

Pass 1, delete on sight:
- em dashes
- "not X but Y", "it's not about X, it's about Y"
- filler bridges: "here's the thing", "let's be honest", "the truth is"
- stock vocabulary: delve, leverage, navigate, unpack, journey, game-changer, landscape, robust, seamless, elevate, empower
- every word on the banned list in my linkedin-post-generator skill
- triplets for rhythm where two or four would be honest
- rhetorical questions the next line answers
- a closing line that says nothing: a slogan, an aphorism, "Thoughts?", a call to action I did not ask for

Pass 2, read as a person:
- one sentence per line where I write paragraphs
- a hook that sounds typed, not said
- a claim with no moment behind it
- hedges where I would just say the thing
- a neat ending that resolves what the post did not

Output: the draft with the changes made, then what was removed, one line each. No prose. No explanations.

Read my linkedin-post-generator skill and copy its banned words into Pass 1 now, so the two lists do not drift. Then propose the skill for saving.
```

---

## Why this is a separate skill

The voice skill is yours and changes every time you reject a line. The AI tells are the same for everyone and change slowly. Keeping them apart means you can rebuild your voice without losing the checklist, and hand the checklist to someone else without handing them your voice.

## Why it runs last

Writing in your voice and checking for AI tells pull in different directions. A single pass that tries both hedges. Two passes, in order, do each job fully.

## What "ready" looks like

- no-ai-slop proposed and saved
- Run it once on the redraft you approved in Step 1. It should change little. If it changes a lot, the voice skill is missing rules. Go back and add them.
